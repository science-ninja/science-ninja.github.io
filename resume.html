Resume

Gregory Fedewa, PhD

Summary

Bioinformatics scientist/Computational biologist. 14+ years experience developing molecular biology and computational methods for high-throughput sequencing/NGS-assays. 4+ years building pipelines. 3+ years designing analytic frameworks to detect & measure biomarkers, and predict disease status & drug response from microbiomes.

Experience

Bioinformatics scientist, Ismagilov Group - Caltech; Pasadena, CA — 2024-2025

  • Built & managed the lab’s Nextflow pipelines (shotgun DNA, 16S, NGS BCL conversion, RNA & DE, MAG assembly).
  • Trained lab members on using best practices in bioinformatics and the HPC.
  • Co-wrote grants submitted to NIH, and DARPA-H.

Bioinformatics scientist, Nuanced Health Inc; Los Angeles, CA — 2021-2024

  • Developed and maintained NGS pipelines, ETL, metagenomic, metaproteomic, and metametabolomic pipelines to be configurable, scalable, and cost effective. These pipelines processed 1000s of samples later analyzed by myself and others.
  • Built predictive models (ML) to demonstrate the discovery platform’s clinical relevance and its potential for biomarker discovery.
  • Analyzed large scale metagenomic microbiome datasets (more than 800 samples) and worked with management team to build a data-driven selection of samples that became the foundation of the company’s discovery platform.
  • Analyzed large scale metagenomic and metaproteomic datasets (100s of samples) to quantify diverse baseline molecular phenotypes of company’s discovery platform, and to find biomarkers of disease & drug response.
  • Presented results of experiments to company, and the board of directors to help them to make data-based decisions.
  • Worked in a team to setup and maintain cloud (AWS & GCS) infrastructure using a Infrastructure as code paradigm (Terraform) to ensure teammates had access to needed resources while following standard security practices.

Postdoctoral researcher, Derek J Smith Lab - Uni of Cambridge; Cambridge, UK — 2018-2021

  • Developed novel predictive algorithms to deconvolute high-dimensional immunological/antigenic data into component signals using multidimensional scaling (deconvoluted antigenic cartography) - used to model subpopulations of antibodies and predict evolving immune responses to evolving antigens.
  • Collaborated on multi-institutional projects using above methods to interpret patterns of immune response and pathogen evolution for dengue virus, and influenza virus.

Phd Candidate, Joseph L DeRisi Lab - UCSF; San Francisco, CA — 2012-2018

  • Designed and implemented a NGS analysis pipeline in Python to quantify viral genome mutation frequencies across virus evolution/time-series experiments.
  • Worked with collaborators on the design of experiments to test and validate viral (Ebola virus) evolution in BSL-4 lab.
  • Demonstrated intra-sample genomic variability of high-priority viral metagenomic samples can be used to correctly identify virus samples and set a baseline for later genomic comparisons at BSL-4 lab.
